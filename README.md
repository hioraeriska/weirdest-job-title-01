## The title

> **Ghostwriter for Pet Autobiographies**

A real job — ghostwriting — with one gloriously specific twist: the clients
are pets, and the books are their autobiographies. Somebody has to sit a
golden retriever down, take dictation, and turn "I ate the homework, then I
ate the couch" into a memoir.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the single title, 35 characters and nothing else, to
`/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), where the sealed
gate checks it is 1–60 characters of UTF-8 text and the blind judge compares
it two at a time against the other entries.

## Run locally

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
